# Approved storefront-neutral visual direction
Studio Pollen approved a vivid, playful collage direction: coral and violet identity shapes may surround product imagery, while embedded captions remain readable and the actual product remains recognizable. No particular storefront is in scope.

Validation V4 passed a small discovery composition with a single short message and a large explanatory composition with detailed product evidence. This is an authoritative validation record, not a claim that this document run rendered assets.

The approved original is pollen-mark.svg, canonical name Pollen Mark. The approved template is collage-template.svg, canonical name Pollen Collage. The template's discovery variant gives priority to recognizing the product; the detail variant gives priority to explaining its benefit. The central blank area is reserved for faithful product imagery; identity shapes stay outside embedded caption areas. Recoloring the original is not approved. These constraints avoid mistaken product identity and obscured captions; there is no general ban on color or decoration. Exact export dimensions, store placements, formats, and palette tokens have not been approved.

Use the original mark by file reference, preserving its two source fills and geometry; do not redraw an approximation. Use the collage template's existing decorative layer and central product region. Product content and caption text may vary with the discovery/detail task, but recoloring the mark, moving identity shapes into caption areas, or inventing an export preset is unsupported.
