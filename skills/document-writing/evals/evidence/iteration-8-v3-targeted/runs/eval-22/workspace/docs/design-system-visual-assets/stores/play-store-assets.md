# Google Play listing artwork

## Store research

Requirements below were checked against the current Google Play Console Help guidance on 2026-08-29. “Required” means a publishing requirement; “highly recommended” guidance primarily affects recommendation and promotional eligibility. Recheck the source before each submission because these requirements can change.

Official source: [Add preview assets to showcase your app](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151?hl=en)

## Required image assets

### App icon

- Required to publish the store listing.
- 32-bit PNG with alpha.
- 512 × 512 px.
- Maximum file size: 1,024 KB.
- Must follow Google Play icon design specifications. Do not include badges or text that implies ranking, price, Google Play categories, or otherwise misleads users.

### Feature graphic

- Required to publish the store listing.
- JPEG or 24-bit PNG, with no alpha.
- 1,024 × 500 px.
- Highly recommended: Keep prominent visuals and the focal point toward the center to avoid UI cutoff zones. Localize graphic or branding text where appropriate.
- Highly recommended: Do not use Google Play or other store badges/icons, unauthorized third-party trademarks, device imagery, or copy suggesting ranking, awards, testimonials, price, or promotions.
- Highly recommended: Provide alt text for each graphic asset; keep the description to 140 characters or fewer and identify the important visual context.

### Screenshots

- Provide at least two screenshots across different device types to publish the store listing.
- JPEG or 24-bit PNG, with no alpha.
- Minimum dimension: 320 px; maximum dimension: 3,840 px. The maximum dimension must not exceed twice the minimum dimension.
- Up to eight screenshots can be added for each supported device type.
- Highly recommended: Screenshots must show the actual in-app or in-game experience. Do not include people interacting with the device unless off-device interaction is the core experience.
- Highly recommended: Provide alt text for each screenshot; keep the description to 140 characters or fewer and identify the important visual context.

## Device-specific image requirements

- **Chromebook and tablets:** Provide at least four screenshots for large-screen presentation; upload images between 1,080 and 7,680 px using 16:9 landscape or 9:16 portrait framing. Avoid extra text that is not part of the core experience.
- **Wear OS apps and watch faces:** Provide at least one current screenshot, showing only the app interface or watch-face experience. Use 1:1 framing at a minimum of 384 × 384 px; do not use device frames, extra text/graphics/backgrounds, transparent backgrounds, or masking.
- **Android TV:** Provide at least one Android TV screenshot before publishing and a banner asset. The banner is JPEG or 24-bit PNG with no alpha at 1,280 × 720 px.
- **Android Automotive OS:** Requirements depend on app category. Where screenshots are required, they must show the generic system UI and accurately represent the Automotive OS experience; do not use vehicle-model- or OEM-specific imagery. If provided, include at least two portrait screenshots (800 × 1,280 px) and two landscape screenshots (1,024 × 768 px).
- **Android XR:** Upload 4–8 screenshots as PNG or JPEG, up to 8 MB each, in an 8:5 aspect ratio. Recommended resolution is 3,840 × 2,400 px; minimum is 1,920 × 1,200 px.

## Recommendation-focused screenshot guidance

These are not base store-listing publishing requirements, but are requirements for recommendation formats that use screenshots:

- **Apps:** at least four screenshots at minimum 1,080 px resolution: 1,920 × 1,080 px minimum for landscape (16:9) or 1,080 × 1,920 px minimum for portrait (9:16).
- **Games:** at least three 16:9 landscape screenshots at 1,920 × 1,080 px minimum, or three 9:16 portrait screenshots at 1,080 × 1,920 px minimum. Show the in-game experience.
- For screenshots with taglines, keep taglines to no more than 20% of the image, avoid calls to action and time-sensitive promotional text, and localize added text for each market.

## Asset roles

Screenshots show the current product experience. Keep any claims shown in the artwork consistent with the listing review package and the actual app or game.
