# Google Play listing artwork

## Store research

This file covers Google Play visual assets for app and game listings. The current first-party source is [Add preview assets to showcase your app](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151), verified 2026-08-29. Google distinguishes mandatory “Requirements” from “Highly recommended” guidance; the latter affects recommendation or promotional eligibility and presentation, not the base listing publication requirement.

## Image requirements

### App icon

Required to publish the store listing.

- 32-bit PNG with alpha
- 512 × 512 px
- Maximum 1,024 KB
- Follow Google Play icon design specifications
- Do not include badges or text suggesting ranking, price, Google Play categories, or another misleading relationship

### Feature graphic

Required to publish the store listing.

- JPEG or 24-bit PNG without alpha
- 1,024 × 500 px
- Keep prominent visuals and the focal point toward the center because some surfaces can crop the edges or add overlays
- Keep background elements toward the edges; avoid pure white, black, or dark gray backgrounds that can blend into Google Play
- Localize graphic or branding text as needed
- Do not include store badges/icons, unlicensed third-party trademarks, device imagery, ranking or award claims, testimonials, price or promotional information, or quickly outdated content
- Include alt text of 140 characters or fewer for each graphic asset

### Screenshots

Required to publish the store listing: at least two screenshots across different device types.

- JPEG or 24-bit PNG without alpha
- Minimum dimension 320 px; maximum dimension 3,840 px
- The maximum dimension must not exceed twice the minimum dimension
- Up to 8 screenshots per supported device type
- Show the actual in-app or in-game experience and keep any added tagline text to a minimum; localize added text for each supported language
- Do not include device imagery, store badges/icons, unlicensed third-party marks, ranking or promotional claims, or misleading depictions
- Include alt text of 140 characters or fewer for each screenshot

Device-specific branches:

- For Chromebooks and 7-inch or 10-inch tablets, provide at least 4 screenshots; upload between 1,080 and 7,680 px and use 16:9 landscape or 9:16 portrait. Additional text outside the core experience can be cut off on some surfaces.
- For Wear OS, provide at least one current app-interface screenshot, without device frames, extra text, graphics, or backgrounds; use 1:1 at least 384 × 384 px, without transparency or masking.
- For Wear OS watch faces, provide at least one current watch-face screenshot and show more than one available permutation when customizable; use the same 1:1 and 384 × 384 px minimum, without frames, extra elements, transparency, or masking.
- For Android TV, provide at least one Android TV screenshot and the required banner asset before publishing an Android TV-enabled app.
- For Android Automotive OS, requirements vary by app category. Non-parked categories require screenshots; parked categories may omit them. If supplied, use generic system UI, not an individual vehicle or OEM. The source specifies at least 2 portrait screenshots (800 × 1,280 px) and 2 landscape screenshots (1,024 × 768 px).
- For Android XR, upload 4–8 PNG or JPEG screenshots, up to 8 MB each, at 8:5; recommended resolution is 3,840 × 2,400 px with a 1,920 × 1,200 px minimum.

For recommendation formats, apps should provide at least four screenshots at 1,080 px minimum (1,920 × 1,080 landscape or 1,080 × 1,920 portrait); games should provide at least three screenshots in one of those orientations and show the in-game experience. These are recommendation-eligibility guidelines, not the base two-screenshot publication requirement.

### Android TV banner

Required only for an Android TV-enabled app and displayed only on Android TV devices.

- JPEG or 24-bit PNG without alpha
- 1,280 × 720 px

## Asset roles

Screenshots show the signed-in product experience. Keep any claims shown in the artwork consistent with the listing review package.
