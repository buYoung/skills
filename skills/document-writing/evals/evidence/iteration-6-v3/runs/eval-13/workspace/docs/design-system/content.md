# Content

## Voice

Use precise, calm, task-oriented language. Labels and instructions should tell the user what will happen or what action is available without promotional flourish or unnecessary urgency.

## Rules

- Name the primary action with a direct verb that matches its outcome.
- Keep supporting explanations concise and adjacent to the control or data they clarify.
- Describe errors with the problem, its impact, and the next useful action.
- Do not rely on color, position, or iconography alone to communicate meaning.
- Preserve domain terminology consistently; unresolved product terminology should be recorded before broad adoption.

## Unresolved decisions

The supplied brief does not define the product's terminology, localization policy, formatting conventions, or validation-message vocabulary. Those decisions remain open and must be owned here when supplied.
