# Orbit Market Publisher Guide

Orbit Market Publishing Ltd. operates Orbit Market for independent productivity apps.

## Discovery artwork

- Every listing needs one 4:5 discovery card. Keep the app name in the lower quarter and leave the top third free of text.
- A listing may include up to six product screenshots. Show the signed-in experience rather than setup or account-creation screens.
- A silent preview of up to 15 seconds is optional. If included, begin with a visible task outcome in the first three seconds.

## Submission notes

The publishing team reviews the discovery card and screenshots together. These rules apply to productivity-app listings only.
