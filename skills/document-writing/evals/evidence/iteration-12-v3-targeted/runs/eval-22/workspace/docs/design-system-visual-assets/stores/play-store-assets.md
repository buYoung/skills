# Google Play listing artwork

## Current image requirements

The following requirements are taken from the official Google Play Console guidance, checked 2026-08-29: [Add preview assets to showcase your app](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151?hl=en).

### App icon

- Required to publish the store listing.
- 32-bit PNG with alpha.
- 512 × 512 px.
- Maximum file size: 1024 KB.
- Follow Google Play’s icon design specifications. Do not include badges or text that imply ranking, price, Google Play categories, or otherwise mislead users.

### Feature graphic

- Required to publish the store listing.
- JPEG or 24-bit PNG with no alpha.
- 1024 × 500 px.

### Screenshots

- Provide at least two screenshots across different device types to publish the store listing.
- JPEG or 24-bit PNG with no alpha.
- Minimum dimension: 320 px.
- Maximum dimension: 3840 px.
- The maximum dimension must not be more than twice the minimum dimension.
- Include screenshots that demonstrate the actual in-app experience and focus on core features and content. Do not include people interacting with the device, such as fingers tapping the screen, unless off-device interaction is core to the experience.
- Include alt text with each screenshot.

For recommendation surfaces, apps should provide at least four screenshots at a minimum 1080 px resolution: 16:9 landscape (1920 × 1080 px minimum) or 9:16 portrait (1080 × 1920 px minimum).

## Asset roles

Screenshots show the signed-in product experience. Keep any claims shown in the artwork consistent with the listing review package.
