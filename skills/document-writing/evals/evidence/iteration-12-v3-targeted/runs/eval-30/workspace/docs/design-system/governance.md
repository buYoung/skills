# Governance

## Direction authority

The approved direction and its representative validation are the current authority for this set. A direction change requires revisiting the common principles and checking at least two contrasting contexts again.

## Review and validation

Reviewers validate the dense web table, short iOS completion flow, large header logo, and 16px feature icon. They record whether the common hierarchy and calm precision remain intact and whether any variation is justified by platform, task, or scale.

## Change ownership

Common visual decisions belong in `foundations.md`; tokens in `tokens.md`; shared patterns in `patterns.md`; and local platform or component differences in their owning files. Conflicts are resolved at the narrowest valid owner unless they alter the approved direction.

## Status and unresolved items

Status: documented from approved direction and supplied validation. Exact token values, accessibility thresholds, and detailed component anatomy remain open implementation decisions and must be recorded by their owners before those contracts are treated as complete.
