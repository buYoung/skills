# Feature icon

## Purpose

Provide quick recognition of a product function. Meaning and legibility take precedence over brand expression.

## Variants and sizes

The 16px feature icon is a validated small-size situation. The complete icon inventory, supported size steps, stroke or fill treatment, and exact geometry are unresolved.

## Behavior and adaptive rules

Icons may simplify at 16px to preserve recognition. States and emphasis may vary with context, but the semantic meaning must remain stable. Use text or accessible labels when the icon alone is insufficient.

## Prohibited usage

Do not add fine detail, decorative motion, or logo-like branding that disappears or confuses at 16px. Do not use an icon as the sole carrier of a critical state.

## Verification

At 16px, the icon remains distinguishable at the intended display conditions and its function is recoverable from its accessible name or adjacent text. It must not compete with the data hierarchy in a dense web table or the next action in an iOS completion flow.
