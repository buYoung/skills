# Foundations

## Direction principles

The visual language is calm and precise. Information hierarchy leads; friendliness appears as restrained corner treatment and purposeful motion. Brand expression supports recognition but must not obscure a task, state, or data relationship.

## Invariant decisions

- Every surface establishes a readable primary, supporting, and secondary hierarchy before adding expression.
- Geometry should feel controlled and precise, with softened corners used as a measured human cue rather than a playful motif.
- Motion communicates change, progress, or completion; it is brief in perception and never the sole carrier of meaning.
- The logo is a brand identifier; feature icons are functional identifiers. They share the direction but not necessarily the same detail, scale, or silhouette.

## Variable and conditional decisions

- Density may be compact in the web administrator data table when row scanning and comparison are primary; it may open up in the iOS completion flow when one next action is primary.
- Logo prominence may increase in a large header, while its surrounding hierarchy remains quiet enough that navigation and task context stay findable.
- Feature icons may simplify at 16px to preserve recognition. Larger sizes may carry more nuance only when it does not change the icon's meaning.
- Platform-native controls and conventions may alter presentation and interaction, as documented in [platforms/web.md](platforms/web.md) and [platforms/ios.md](platforms/ios.md).

## Prohibited expression

- Do not use decoration, exuberant motion, or visual noise that competes with the primary task or dense data hierarchy.
- Do not force one corner radius, density, or level of detail onto every context.
- Do not make color, motion, or logo presence the only way to understand status or action.

## Verification criteria

Reviewers should be able to identify the primary action or data relationship immediately in both the dense table and short completion flow. The large header logo should feel recognizably branded without becoming the page's competing task. The 16px feature icon should remain distinguishable without textural detail. Reduced-motion and non-color cues must preserve these outcomes.
