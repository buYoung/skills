# Patterns

## Cross-context composition

Patterns preserve the invariant direction while adapting density and emphasis to the task. A data-comparison pattern may be compact and scan-oriented; a completion pattern may reduce competing choices and make the next action prominent.

## States and transitions

States must be understandable without motion. Motion may confirm a transition or completion when it supports the user's mental model. iOS completion flows may use platform-native transition conventions; web administrator tables may use restrained state changes that do not interrupt scanning.

## Brand and function

Use the logo when the reader needs product identity or orientation. Use feature icons when the reader needs quick functional recognition. Do not substitute the logo for an icon or make a functional icon carry brand detail that harms recognition at 16px.

## Adaptive rule

Keep hierarchy, meaning, and restrained friendliness common. Allow density, scale, interaction presentation, and motion behavior to vary only under the platform and size conditions documented by the owning files.
