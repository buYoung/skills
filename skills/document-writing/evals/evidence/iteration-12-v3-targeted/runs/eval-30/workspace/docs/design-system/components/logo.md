# Logo

## Purpose

Identify and orient the product. The logo is brand-led, not a substitute for a functional icon or task label.

## Variants and sizes

The large header logo is a validated representative situation. Exact geometry, clear space, color treatment, and additional variants are unresolved and must be supplied before implementation binding.

## Adaptive rules

The logo may become more prominent in a large header, but surrounding information hierarchy remains primary. It may simplify at smaller sizes only if recognition and accessible naming are preserved.

## Accessibility and prohibited usage

Provide an appropriate accessible name when the logo conveys identity or navigation. Do not stretch, decorate, animate for attention, or use the logo as a feature icon.

## Verification

At large header scale, the logo should be immediately recognizable without competing with the page's primary task. At smaller scale, it should remain legible without forcing brand detail that harms hierarchy.
