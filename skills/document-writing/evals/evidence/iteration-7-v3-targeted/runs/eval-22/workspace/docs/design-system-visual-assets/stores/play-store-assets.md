# Google Play listing artwork

## Scope and research status

This file covers visual assets for a Google Play store listing. The requirements below were checked against Google's first-party Play Console Help guidance on 2026-08-29. “Required” means mandatory for publishing or for the named device/storefront condition; “recommended” is guidance for discovery or promotion and is not a base submission requirement.

Official source: [Add preview assets to showcase your app](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151?hl=en) (verified 2026-08-29).

## Required image assets

| Asset | Status | Requirements | Applicability |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| App icon | Required to publish the listing | 32-bit PNG with alpha; 512 × 512 px; maximum 1,024 KB; must meet Google Play icon design specifications | Main listing |
| Feature graphic | Required to publish the listing | JPEG or 24-bit PNG, no alpha; 1,024 × 500 px | Main listing; may also be used as a preview-video cover |
| Screenshots | Required to publish the listing | At least 2 screenshots across different device types; JPEG or 24-bit PNG, no alpha; each dimension at least 320 px and at most 3,840 px; the maximum dimension must not exceed twice the minimum dimension | Main listing |
| Android TV banner | Required when the app is Android TV-enabled | JPEG or 24-bit PNG, no alpha; 1,280 × 720 px | Android TV only |

The app icon is the higher-fidelity, higher-resolution Play listing icon and does not replace the launcher icon. Do not put ranking, price, category, or other potentially misleading badges or text in it.

For device-specific listings, Google Play supports up to 8 screenshots per supported device type. Chromebook and tablet listings can use 4 or more screenshots at 1,080–7,680 px, with 16:9 landscape or 9:16 portrait framing. Wear OS listings require at least one current-version screenshot showing only the app interface, without device frames, additional text, graphics, or non-interface backgrounds; use a 1:1 aspect ratio and at least 384 × 384 px. Android TV listings require at least one Android TV screenshot in addition to the banner. Android Automotive OS and Android XR have additional conditional screenshot rules; confirm the applicable device category before submission.

## Content and delivery checks

- Screenshots must show the actual in-app experience. Keep claims in the artwork consistent with the listing review package and the current product.
- Do not include Google Play or other store badges/icons, unlicensed third-party characters or logos, or device imagery that can quickly become obsolete.
- Keep key content away from feature-graphic cutoff zones, and avoid fine detail that will disappear on smaller screens. Localize graphic and branding text for supported markets.
- Provide alt text for every icon, feature graphic, and screenshot; keep each description to 140 characters or fewer and describe the important context without starting with “photo of” or “image of”.
- For recommendation surfaces, Google recommends (and makes eligibility a requirement for those surfaces) at least 4 app screenshots at 1,080 px minimum, using 1,920 × 1,080 px landscape or 1,080 × 1,920 px portrait; games require at least 3 screenshots in one of those orientations. This is separate from the base requirement to publish a listing.
