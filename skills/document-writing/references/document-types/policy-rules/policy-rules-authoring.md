# Authoring policy or rules

## Required context

Identify the governing authority, audience, covered activities, effective date, owner, obligations, exceptions, enforcement model, and related higher-order requirements.

## Drafting sequence

1. State the governing rule in plain language.
2. Define scope, audience, and exclusions.
3. Define terms that affect compliance.
4. Separate required, permitted, and prohibited behavior.
5. Assign responsibilities to named roles.
6. Define exceptions and approval authority.
7. Define reporting, escalation, or enforcement when applicable.
8. Add effective date, owner, and revision process.
9. Link procedures without embedding unnecessary operational detail.

## Normative language

Use "must" for requirements, "must not" for prohibitions, "may" for permission, and "should" only for recommendations that allow justified deviation. Do not alternate terms for stylistic variety.

## Avoid

- Aspirational statements presented as enforceable rules
- Undefined scope such as "all sensitive data" without a definition
- Exceptions without an approving authority
- A long procedure disguised as policy
