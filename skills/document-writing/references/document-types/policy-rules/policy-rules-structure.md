# Policy or rules structure

## Default structure

1. Title and policy statement
2. Purpose
3. Scope and audience
4. Definitions
5. Required, permitted, and prohibited behavior
6. Roles and responsibilities
7. Exception request and approval process
8. Compliance, reporting, or escalation
9. Approval and effective status, effective date, owner, and revision history
10. Related procedures or references

## Section responsibilities

Keep enforceable rules in the policy. Put detailed step-by-step execution in a linked procedure or guide unless the steps are themselves mandatory policy controls.

Place each rule's exceptions beside the rule they qualify and identify who can approve them, because a reader who looks up one rule may never reach a separate exceptions section. Use the Exceptions section for the general request and approval process and for exceptions that apply across rules. Do not leave enforcement or ownership implicit when either matters.
