# Reviewing policy or rules

## Compliance test

A covered reader should be able to determine whether the policy applies, what they must or must not do, who owns each responsibility, and how exceptions work.

## Findings to surface

- Governing authority, scope, audience, or owner is missing.
- Normative strength changes without reason.
- A term that controls compliance is undefined.
- Requirements conflict or contain hidden exceptions.
- Exception approval is unowned.
- A procedure is embedded without clarifying whether every step is mandatory.
- The effective date or revision status is unclear.
- The artifact is actually a guide, proposal, or FDD.

## Quality bar

The policy should be enforceable and understandable without relying on oral context or inconsistent interpretations.
