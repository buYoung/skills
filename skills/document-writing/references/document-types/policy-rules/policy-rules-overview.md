# Policy or rules

## Purpose

Define durable obligations, permissions, prohibitions, exceptions, and ownership so a group can act consistently.

## Select this type when

- The artifact governs repeated behavior over time.
- Readers need to know what is required, allowed, prohibited, or exempted.
- Scope, roles, exception authority, and effective dates matter.

## Route elsewhere when

- The artifact tells readers how to complete one task: action guide.
- It defines one product feature's full behavior and design decisions: FDD.
- It records what participants agreed in one event: record or meeting notes.
- It is a proposal seeking approval for a future policy rather than the policy text itself: proposal.
