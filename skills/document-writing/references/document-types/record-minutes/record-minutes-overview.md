# Record or meeting notes

## Purpose

Preserve what happened, who participated, what was said or agreed, and what remains open so later readers can reconstruct the event without rewriting history.

## Select this type when

- The primary value is a traceable past record.
- Chronology, attribution, agreements, and unresolved items matter.
- Interpretation should remain limited and clearly labeled.

## Route elsewhere when

- Evidence is analyzed to produce findings: analysis report.
- A new approval is requested: proposal.
- Current durable rules are established: policy or rules.
- A single feature's current design decisions must govern implementation: FDD.
