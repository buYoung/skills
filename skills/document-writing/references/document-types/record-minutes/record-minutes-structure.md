# Record or meeting notes structure

## Default structure

1. Title
2. Date, time, location, or channel
3. Participants and roles
4. Purpose or context
5. Concise summary
6. Chronological or topic-based record
7. Decisions and agreements
8. Action items, when requested
9. Open questions and follow-up
10. Attachments or source links

## Responsibility boundaries

### Record

Capture material statements, events, and evidence without turning them into recommendations.

### Decisions and agreements

Record what was actually agreed, by whom, and any effective condition. Do not infer consensus from silence.

### Action items

Include owner and due date only when known. Mark missing ownership rather than inventing it.

## Ordering

Use chronology when sequence matters. Use topic grouping when discussion order would obscure meaning, while preserving timestamps or attribution needed for traceability.
