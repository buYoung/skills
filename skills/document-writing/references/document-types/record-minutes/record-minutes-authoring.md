# Authoring a record or meeting notes

## Required context

Identify the event, date, participants, source material, purpose, required level of attribution, decisions, action items, and confidentiality constraints.

## Drafting sequence

1. Establish event metadata and source coverage.
2. Write a short factual summary.
3. Organize the record by chronology or topic.
4. Separate discussion from decisions.
5. Record action items only with supported owners and dates.
6. Preserve unresolved disagreement and open questions.
7. Link supporting material.
8. Mark paraphrase, inference, or missing source coverage when material.

## Fidelity rules

- Preserve exact quotations when quoted.
- Do not convert tentative discussion into agreement.
- Distinguish proposed, accepted, rejected, deferred, and superseded items.
- Retain corrections or later updates without erasing the earlier record when history matters.
- Avoid editorial judgments unless the user asks for analysis.

## Avoid

- Polishing away disagreement
- Attributing statements without evidence
- Inventing owners or deadlines
- Replacing a chronological record with a retrospective recommendation
