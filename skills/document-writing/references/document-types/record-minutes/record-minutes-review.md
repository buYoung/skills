# Reviewing a record or meeting notes

## Reconstruction test

A reader who was absent should be able to identify what occurred, who was involved, what was decided, what remains open, and where supporting evidence lives.

## Findings to surface

- Event metadata or source coverage is missing.
- Discussion is presented as a decision.
- Attribution is unsupported or inconsistent.
- Chronology has been lost where sequence affects meaning.
- Action items lack known owners or dates without being marked incomplete.
- Corrections erase rather than amend history.
- Analysis or recommendations overshadow the record.
- The artifact is actually a report, policy, proposal, or FDD.

## Quality bar

The record should be concise enough to use and faithful enough to trust.
