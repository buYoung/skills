# Reviewing an analysis report

## Evidence test

A reader should be able to trace each major conclusion to findings and understand the scope, method, and limitations that control confidence.

## Findings to surface

- The analytical question or time boundary is missing.
- The executive summary overstates the evidence.
- Facts, interpretations, and recommendations are mixed.
- Findings use inconsistent baselines or denominators.
- Causal claims exceed the method.
- Limitations are generic or omit a material gap.
- Supporting detail obscures the main narrative.
- The artifact is actually a proposal or a record.

## Quality bar

The report should make the evidence easier to understand without making it appear stronger than it is.
