# Reviewing a proposal

## Decision-maker test

A reader should be able to identify the requested decision, understand why it matters, compare credible options, and see the consequences of accepting or rejecting the recommendation.

## Findings to surface

- The decision request is absent or vague.
- The problem statement assumes the preferred solution.
- Decision criteria are unstated or applied inconsistently.
- Alternatives are strawmen.
- Costs, risks, dependencies, or reversibility are missing.
- Estimates are presented as verified facts.
- The content is primarily a report or an FDD rather than a proposal.
- The ending does not state the next commitment.

## Quality bar

A proposal succeeds by making a real decision easier, not by making the recommendation sound inevitable.
