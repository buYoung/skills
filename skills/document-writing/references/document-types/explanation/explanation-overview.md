# Explanatory overview

## Purpose

Help a reader understand a concept, system, policy rationale, change, or context without requiring them to perform a procedure or look up a formal contract.

## Select this type when

- The reader needs a mental model or concise orientation.
- The document answers what something is, why it matters, and how its parts relate.
- The content should be readable from beginning to end while still supporting scanning.

## Route elsewhere when

- Exact fields, allowed values, or normative contracts dominate: reference or specification.
- Ordered actions dominate: action guide.
- Evidence and findings dominate: analysis report.
- One product feature's design decisions must govern implementation: FDD.
