# Reviewing a reference or specification

## Contract test

A reader should be able to locate an item by its known name and determine its exact meaning, constraints, allowed values, defaults, and exceptions without searching unrelated prose.

## Findings to surface

- Scope or supported version is missing.
- The same term has multiple definitions.
- A required field is omitted for some comparable items.
- Normative language is inconsistent.
- Examples imply a rule not stated in the contract.
- Defaults, units, or failure states are ambiguous.
- Cross-references or identifiers do not resolve.
- The artifact is actually an overview, guide, policy, or FDD.

## Quality bar

Favor unambiguous lookup and verified completeness over stylistic variety. Every material exception should be discoverable from the item it affects.
