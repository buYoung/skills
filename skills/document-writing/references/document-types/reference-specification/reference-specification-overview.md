# Reference or specification

## Purpose

Provide an exact, stable, non-linear lookup surface for definitions, fields, options, states, rules, interfaces, or contracts.

## Select this type when

- Readers will jump directly to a named item.
- Precision and completeness matter more than narrative flow.
- Repeated entities share exact fields or dimensions.
- The document defines a schema, API, configuration, status model, or controlled vocabulary.

## Route elsewhere when

- The reader needs a conceptual introduction: explanatory overview.
- The reader must follow a sequence: action guide.
- The document defines one product feature holistically for implementation: FDD.
- Durable organizational obligations dominate: policy or rules.
